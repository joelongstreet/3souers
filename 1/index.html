Built for Us

Trois Sœurs is a small, ongoing project built by three sisters - Jane, Ruby, and Lucy, and their parents. Together, we create simple structures designed to invite pause. Places where someone might stop for a moment, take something in, and maybe leave something behind. These installations are small by design, but they aim to create space for connection. Between neighbors, between strangers, and sometimes just within yourself.

This library sits in our own front yard. It was built for our family, but it quickly became something shared. Over time, it has turned into a small gathering point. A place where the girls have met friends, where neighbors linger, and where passing moments stretch just a little longer.

We find all kinds of things inside. Books, mostly, but also small surprises. Stuffed animals, handwritten notes, bits of music. Each one left by someone passing through, each one adding to the quiet rhythm of the place.

More than anything, we enjoy watching how people use it. Someone stopping to look. Someone sitting on the wall for a minute. Someone taking something, or leaving something behind. Small moments, happening over and over again.
