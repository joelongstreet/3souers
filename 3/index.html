Built for Louanne Hein

Trois Sœurs is a small, ongoing project built by three sisters - Jane, Ruby, and Lucy, and their parents. Together, we create simple structures designed to invite pause. Places where someone might stop for a moment, take something in, and maybe leave something behind. These installations are small by design, but they aim to create space for connection. Between neighbors, between strangers, and sometimes just within yourself.

This library is dedicated to Louanne Hein, Ruby's art teacher and a former school educator who now spends her Saturdays sharing her time, knowledge, and care. What she offers goes far beyond instruction. Art, in her hands, becomes a way of bringing feelings into the open. A way of giving shape to things that are often hard to name. Not just how to draw or paint, but how to express what lives underneath.

This library is a small gesture in return for what Louanne has given. Her impact is not loud or transactional. It is built over time through patience, attention, and genuine care. The kind of work that stays with someone and continues long after the lesson ends.

We hope this little box of books carries a bit of that same spirit.
