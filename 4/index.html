Built for Grandma Mary and Papa Mark

Trois Sœurs is a small, ongoing project built by three sisters - Jane, Ruby, and Lucy, and their parents. Together, we create simple structures designed to invite pause. Places where someone might stop for a moment, take something in, and maybe leave something behind. These installations are small by design, but they aim to create space for connection. Between neighbors, between strangers, and sometimes just within yourself.

This library was made possible by Mark and Mary Jordahl, the grandparents of the three sisters. Their presence in the girls' lives is steady and unmistakable. The role of a grandparent carries a different rhythm. It is slower, more patient, and often a little more forgiving. It leaves room for each child to be met as they are.

Mark and Mary have a way of doing just that. They bring care, honesty, and a sense of fun, adjusting naturally to each of their grandchildren. Sometimes that looks like guidance, sometimes like listening, and sometimes like simply being there.

This library stands as a reflection of that kind of presence. Quiet, consistent, and shaped by attention to others.
