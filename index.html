Built together.

Trois Sœurs is French for three sisters. We're Jane, Ruby, and Lucy, plus a mom, a dad, and a dog named Rufus who mostly just supervises. We build things together, out of a little shop, with help from friends when we need it.

Something happens when you build things with your hands alongside people you love. We're still figuring out exactly what it is, but we keep showing up anyway.

What we're currently making

Right now, it's little libraries. The kind you find on a corner and stop at for just a second to peek inside. We build them to live outside, collect some weather, and give people a small excuse to slow down.

Each one ends up belonging to wherever it lands.

How we work together

We have roles, loosely. Mostly we just do whatever needs doing. Some days that's painting and debating colors. Other days it's measuring things twice and cutting once, and it comes out exactly right.

It shifts around. That's kind of the whole point.
