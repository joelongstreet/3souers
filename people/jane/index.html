Hard Things

Written by Jane, age 10

I built a little library because I could not read at first and it was really hard to do things - like tests at school and homework.

I was really struggling.

So me and my family wanted something that could help me read, but at the same time stand out in a unique way. We thought a lot about and we thought a little library is the perfect thing.

Now kids can practice reading at a fun place. I meet a lot of my friends there and I think you will too!
